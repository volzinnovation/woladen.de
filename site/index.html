About woladen.de

Find available chargers near great bakeries, restaurants, shops, playgrounds and cafés. woladen knows ... stations with ... charging points in many countries and shows them within 20 km of your location or selected map position. Because charging time should be time well spent. By default, woladen searches fast chargers from 50 kW; the power filter can include normal chargers below 50 kW.
